和の心

Bringing the heart
of Japan to the world,
and to the future.

Noriyuki Ikeda  /  Kimono merchant, Japan

Message

A thread worth reconnecting

Cleaning, wearing a kimono, making a bowl of tea.
For centuries, the Japanese have polished the heart through the small things right in front of them.

This wisdom used to pass naturally from grandmother to grandchild.
Today, even in Japan, that thread is wearing thin.

Through talks and writing, I hope to reconnect it —
and share it with anyone, anywhere, who feels drawn to it.

Words

Words you will meet here

心

Kokoro

The heart, the mind and the spirit — all in one word.

和

Wa

Harmony. Also simply “Japanese,” as in wa-fuku (Japanese clothing).

着物

Kimono

Literally “a thing to wear.” Cut in straight lines from one bolt of cloth.

道

Dō

“The way.” A practice that polishes the heart: sadō (tea), budō (martial arts), sōji-dō (cleaning).
