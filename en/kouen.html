Talks

Talks

Kimono, cleaning, tea and habits — everyday subjects that open a window onto the Japanese heart.
Each talk is shaped for its audience: business groups, schools and local communities.

Themes

Main themes

01

Change your habits, change your life

Why we give up after three days, and how to keep going by splitting the first month into three. From his own experience of losing 25 kg.

02

The toilet and the Japanese

The feeling that gods dwell in all things, and the Japanese wisdom of polishing the heart through cleaning — from myth to the “way of cleaning.”

03

Tea etiquette, in kimono

How to eat the sweets, how to use kaishi paper. The care for others hidden in each gesture, learned by doing.

04

Kimono and the Japanese heart

Cut in straight lines and returned to a single bolt when unstitched — what the kimono says about living with nature and caring for things.

Talks are given in Japanese. If you would like English support, please tell us when you get in touch.
