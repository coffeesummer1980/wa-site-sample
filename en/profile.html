Kimono merchant

Noriyuki Ikeda

池田訓之 · IKEDA NORIYUKI

A kimono merchant. Born in Kyoto in 1962 and a graduate of Doshisha University's Faculty of Law, he spent ten years preparing for Japan's bar exam before entering the kimono trade at 33. He now runs Wasoukan, a group of kimono stores in Tottori and Shimane, and shares the theme “Bringing the Japanese heart to the world, and to the future” through talks, radio and writing.

His motto is “Through kimono, bring the Japanese heart to the world and to the future.” Beyond kimono, he talks about cleaning, tea and everyday Japanese customs — the small practices through which the Japanese have long polished the heart. He is also a licensed teacher of kimono dressing and of the Enshu school of tea ceremony, and an instructor with Silva Method Japan (SMC).
